About

Some history, some obsessions, and where I am now.

My training in logic gave me the underlying skeleton of how I think. Years as an EdTech product manager taught me how to turn the abstract into the usable. And now I spend my energy on one thing: making AI genuinely useful — not as a demo, but in production.

I've solved operations research problems in Python and simulated group behavior with agent-based modeling. Looking back, those seemingly scattered experiments were all training the same skill: spotting structure inside complex problems, and carrying methods across domains.

Symbolic logic, optimization constraints, close reading, narrative games — I've dived into many fields, and every cross-disciplinary deep dive has quietly widened my boundaries.

Right now, I'm exploring what AI and education can become together. With one hand I build products from 0 to 1; with the other I craft content that demands taste and a sense of story. Increasingly, I believe what's truly scarce is neither technology nor aesthetics themselves — but the pathway between the two.
