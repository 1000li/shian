AI Literacy Course

Content / education · Taught live to 200+ students

Product

Good material for AI literacy is scarce, so I designed a course that runs from first principles to real applications to ethical reflection. The 30,000-word lecture notes and field-study handbook have been taught live to 200+ students, whose feedback was “finally, AI that makes sense” — the goal isn't technical minutiae, it's giving non-technical people a framework for thinking about AI.

Engineering

Three-layer progression: AI fundamentals (LLMs / neural networks) → industry cases (education / healthcare / creative work) → AI ethics and social impact. The companion field-study handbook includes interactive exercises calibrated to middle-school reading level, and the content keeps iterating after release.
